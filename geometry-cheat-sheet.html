Lines & Transversals Cheat Code

Unit 1

Big Angle / Small Angle Rule: All Big (obtuse) angles are equal, all Small (acute) angles are equal. Any Big + Small = 180°.

• Corresponding Angles: Equal (Same spot, shifted down)
• Alternate Interior: Equal (Inside tracks, opposite sides)
• Alternate Exterior: Equal (Outside tracks, opposite sides)
• Consecutive Interior: Add up to 180° (Inside tracks, same side)

Top 6 Proof Reasons & Properties

Unit 2
Reflexive Property: AB ≅ AB or ∠A ≅ ∠A

Anything equals itself (used when triangles share a side).

Transitive Property:

If A = B and B = C, then A = C.

CPCTC:

Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles are Congruent. (Use AFTER proving ≅ triangles).

Vertical Angles Theorem:

Angles across from each other at an X intersection are ALWAYS congruent.
