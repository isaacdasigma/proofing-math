Unit 2: Two-Column Proofs Without Panic

Master two-column proofs like a detective mystery: Statements are your clues, Reasons are your rules of law.

Mental Model

1. The "Detective Mystery" System

Two-column proofs scare high schoolers because teachers grade them so strictly. But every single proof follows the exact same 3-step pattern:

Step 1
The "Given" Clues

Always write down what the problem gives you first. Free points on every test!

Step 2
The Hidden Links

Look for shared sides (Reflexive) or vertical X-angles to create new true facts.

Step 3
The Final Verdict

Finish with the "Prove" line using SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, HL, or CPCTC.

2. The "Top 6" Reasons Used in 90% of Proofs

Memorize these 6 rules, and you will be able to solve almost any high school geometry proof:

Reason Name When to Use It Example Statement
Reflexive Property Two triangles share the exact same side line. AC ≅ AC
Vertical Angles Thm Two straight lines cross making an X shape. ∠1 ≅ ∠2
Def. of Midpoint Given says "M is the midpoint of AB". AM ≅ MB
Def. of Angle Bisector Given says "BD bisects ∠ABC". ∠ABD ≅ ∠DBC
Alternate Interior Angles Given mentions parallel lines (AB ∥ CD). ∠BAC ≅ ∠DCA
CPCTC AFTER you proved △ABC ≅ △CDA to prove leftover sides/angles equal. BC ≅ DA
