Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Your privacy and safety on Proofing Math matter to us.

1. Overview

Welcome to Proofing Math ("we," "our," or "us"). Proofing Math is a free, student-focused educational resource designed to help high school students master Geometry, Algebra, and AMDM. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you visit our site.

2. Information We Collect

Proofing Math does not require account creation, registration, or login. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, postal addresses, or phone numbers.

Log Files & Device Data:

Like most standard website servers, we automatically gather non-personal usage information when you browse our site. This may include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, platform type, date/time stamps, and number of clicks to analyze trends and administer the site.

4. Children & Student Privacy Protection

Proofing Math is built for high school students. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data on our site, please contact us immediately so we can remove such information.
