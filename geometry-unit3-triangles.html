Unit 3: Triangles & Congruence

Master the 5 valid congruence shortcuts (SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, HL), dodge the dangerous SSA and AAA traps, and conquer triangle angle theorems with confidence.

Core Shortcuts

1. The 5 Valid Triangle Congruence Shortcuts

To prove two triangles are identical in shape and size (△ABC ≅ △DEF), you do not need to measure all 3 sides and all 3 angles. You only need one of these 5 trusted rules:

SSS Side-Side-Side

All 3 corresponding pairs of sides are equal in length.

AB ≅ DE, BC ≅ EF, AC ≅ DF
SAS Side-Angle-Side

2 sides and the included angle (the angle pinched right between them).

AB ≅ DE, ∠B ≅ ∠E, BC ≅ EF
ASA Angle-Side-Angle

2 angles and the included side (the side connecting the two angles).

∠A ≅ ∠D, AB ≅ DE, ∠B ≅ ∠E
AAS Angle-Angle-Side

2 angles and a non-included side (a side not directly between the two angles).

∠A ≅ ∠D, ∠B ≅ ∠E, BC ≅ EF
HL Hypotenuse-Leg
Right Triangles Only

Requires 3 strict conditions: (1) Right Triangles (90° angle present), (2) Congruent Hypotenuses, and (3) One pair of congruent legs.

∠C = ∠F = 90°, AB ≅ DE (Hypotenuse), AC ≅ DF (Leg)

Exam Traps

2. The Two Dangerous Traps (SSA & AAA)

Teachers love to put these two combinations on tests to trick you. Neither of these guarantees triangle congruence!

⚠️

SSA / ASS (The "Swinging Door")

Side-Side-Angle does NOT prove congruence! Because the angle is not trapped between the sides, the second side can "swing" into two completely different triangle shapes (the ambiguous case).

💡 Memory Rule: "Don't be an ASS on a Geometry test — it won't work!" (Unless it's HL in a right triangle).
📐

AAA (Angle-Angle-Angle)

AAA proves that two triangles have the same shape (they are Similar ~), but NOT that they are the same size!

💡 Example: A tiny 30°-60°-90° plastic ruler vs. a giant 30°-60°-90° billboard triangle. Same angles, vastly different sizes!

3. Isosceles & Equilateral Triangle Rules

Key rules for solving missing side lengths and angle measures:

△

Isosceles Triangle Theorem (Base Angles Theorem)

If two sides of a triangle are congruent (AB ≅ AC), then the angles opposite those sides are also congruent (∠B ≅ ∠C).

Converse is also true: Equal base angles ➔ Equal opposite legs.
60°

Equilateral & Equiangular Property

If all 3 sides are congruent, then all 3 interior angles are congruent and equal to 60° (since 180° / 3 = 60°).

∠x

Exterior Angle Theorem

The measure of an exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two remote (opposite) interior angles: ∠Exterior = ∠A + ∠B.
