Core Formulas

1. Circle & Sector ("Pizza Slice") Formulas

A sector is just a fraction of the full circle. If you know the angle (θ) out of 360°, you just multiply the full circle formula by (θ / 360°):

Entire Circle
• Area: A = πr²
• Circumference: C = 2πr (or πd)
Fraction of Circle (Sector)
• Sector Area: (θ / 360°) × πr²
• Arc Length: (θ / 360°) × 2πr

2. Central vs. Inscribed Angle Rule

Where the vertex of the angle sits inside the circle changes its angle measure completely:

Rule 1

Central Angle (Vertex at Center)

The angle measure is EXACTLY EQUAL to its intercepted arc.
Example: Central Angle = 80° → Arc = 80°

Rule 2

Inscribed Angle (Vertex on Edge)

The angle measure is HALF of its intercepted arc.
Example: Arc = 80° → Inscribed Angle = 40°
