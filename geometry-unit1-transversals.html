Unit 1: Lines & Transversals

Master parallel line angle rules using the 10-second "Big Angle / Small Angle" shortcut without memorizing confusing jargon.

Core Shortcut

1. The "Big Angle / Small Angle" Rule

When a line (the transversal) cuts across two parallel lines, it makes 8 angles. Textbooks try to force you to learn 5 different definitions, but visually it's crazy simple:

⚡ The 10-Second Rule:

  • 1. Every Big Angle (Obtuse) is EQUAL to every other Big Angle.
  • 2. Every Small Angle (Acute) is EQUAL to every other Small Angle.
  • 3. Any 1 Big Angle + 1 Small Angle = 180° (Supplementary).

2. The Jargon Translator

Teachers will test you on the official math names. Here is how to translate textbook terms into plain English:

Textbook Term Plain English Meaning Are They Equal?
Corresponding Same corner position, just moved down line. YES (Equal)
Alternate Interior Opposite sides of transversal, INSIDE parallel lines. YES (Equal)
Alternate Exterior Opposite sides of transversal, OUTSIDE parallel lines. YES (Equal)
Vertical Angles Across from each other at the exact same "X". YES (Equal)
Consecutive Interior Same side of transversal, INSIDE parallel lines. NO! (Add to 180°)
