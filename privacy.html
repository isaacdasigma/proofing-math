Welcome to Proofing Math. We build free, student-focused high school math tools and interactive lessons across Geometry, Algebra, and AMDM. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled when you browse our website.

Standard non-identifying technical data (such as browser type, device type, referring pages, and date/time stamps) may be logged automatically by host servers to maintain website reliability and performance.
