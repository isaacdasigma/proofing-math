Core Formulas

1. Polygon Interior & Exterior Angle Formulas

Any polygon with n sides can be divided into (n - 2) interior triangles. Because every triangle has 180°, we get these core rules:

Interior Sum
(n - 2) × 180°
Total sum of all inside angles
One Regular Angle
[(n - 2) × 180°] / n
Each angle in an equal polygon
Exterior Sum
ALWAYS 360°
Outside angles always add to 360°

2. The Quadrilateral Family Tree

Properties flow downwards: anything true for a Parallelogram is automatically true for Rectangles, Rhombuses, and Squares!

Parallelogram (The Parent)

  • • Opposite sides are parallel and equal in length
  • • Opposite angles are equal; consecutive angles add to 180°
  • • Diagonals bisect (cut each other in half)

Rectangle

All 4 angles are 90°. Diagonals are congruent (equal in length).

Rhombus

All 4 sides are equal. Diagonals intersect at perpendicular 90° angles.

Square (The Ultimate Combo)

Combines ALL properties of both a Rectangle and a Rhombus! 4 equal sides, 4 right angles, and 90° bisecting equal diagonals.
